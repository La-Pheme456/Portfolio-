01 / About

More than just code.

I'm interested in the space between technology and creativity — where a website becomes more than a collection of pages.

I don't just want to make websites that work. I want to make websites worth remembering.

I'm Lawal Oluwafemi, professionally known as LA-PHÈMÉ, a passionate web developer with around five years of experience building and designing for the web.

My current foundation is built around HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and Vanilla JavaScript. These technologies have taught me the fundamentals of how the web works and how to translate ideas into usable interfaces.

But I'm not interested in remaining comfortable. I'm continuously expanding toward MERN Stack development, DevOps and cybersecurity.

Video editing is another part of my creative toolkit. It has influenced how I think about timing, composition, movement and visual storytelling.
