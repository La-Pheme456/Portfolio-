01 / About

More than just code.

I'm interested in the space between technology and creativity — where a website becomes more than a collection of pages.

I don't just want to make websites that work. I want to make websites worth remembering.

I'm Lawal Oluwafemi, professionally known as LA-PHÈMÉ. I enjoy taking an idea that starts as a simple thought and turning it into something people can actually use.

My foundation is built around HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and Vanilla JavaScript. Learning these tools taught me that good websites are not only about writing code. They are also about making things clear, useful and easy to understand.

I also know I still have a lot to learn. That is part of what keeps me interested. I'm growing toward MERN Stack development, DevOps and cybersecurity, one step at a time.

Video editing is another part of my creative side. It has changed the way I look at timing, movement and visual storytelling. I bring that same thinking into the websites I build.
